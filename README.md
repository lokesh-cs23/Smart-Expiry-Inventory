Smart Expiry Management System with Automated Email Alerts
Overview

The Smart Expiry Management System is a web-based application designed to help users track and manage the expiry dates of products such as groceries, medicines, and household items. The system automatically monitors expiry dates and sends email notifications when products are nearing expiration, helping users reduce wastage and improve inventory management.

Features
Add, update, delete, and manage product details
Track product expiry dates efficiently
Automated email alerts for expiring products
Dashboard displaying product statistics
Categorized product management
Responsive and user-friendly interface
PostgreSQL database integration
Automated expiry monitoring using Cron Jobs

How It Works
Users add product details including name, category, quantity, expiry date, and email address.
Product information is stored securely in the PostgreSQL database.
A background scheduler continuously checks product expiry dates.
When a product is close to its expiry date, the system automatically sends an email alert.
Users can view all product information and expiry statistics through the dashboard.
